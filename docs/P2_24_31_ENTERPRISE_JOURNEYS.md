# Experience Center — connected enterprise stories, without fabricated results

| Story | Business challenge & personas | User journey and WSO2 controls | Evidence / stakeholder / KPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aurora retail returns** | Customers expect seamless service across browser/mobile; customer Ana and Aurora seller Marcos | Real tenant-qualified OIDC code+PKCE; customer submits owned request; seller uses WSO2 `ciam:cases:review`; approval requires real fresh TOTP, distinct reviewer and verified token scope. No partner identity automatically receives seller scopes. | **Baseline:** BFF issuer/subject, live introspection, case audit; **P2 MFA-positive PENDING**. CX & fraud lead. KPI: approval turnaround, false-denial rate, unauthorized write count. |
| **High-value financial-style approval** | Sensitive action must resist stolen sessions; seller/reviewer | Conditional login policy for sensitive app or risk condition; freshly authenticated `amr`, `auth_time`, `acr` read only from signed WSO2 ID token; backend rejects stale/missing proof; retry is a *new* authentication transaction. | **PENDING:** real policy config, fresh TOTP success and stale negative. Security/Compliance. KPI: verified step-up completion, denied stale approvals, median friction. |
| **Aurora Returns & Fulfillment Partner** | Outside partner manages its own agents without touching Aurora root or Brisa | Aurora root creates child via narrow org API; shares approved application/roles; partner-scoped switched token edits its own SCIM users; business API checks delegated subject+org+scope. Offboarding removes access with token/session tests. | **PENDING live:** guarded source provisioner included, parent/sibling/Brisa negative probes and UI still required. Partner Ops. KPI: onboarding time, time-to-revoke, cross-org authorization failures. |
| **Brisa federated account** | Enterprise partner needs SSO but not cross-brand access | Controlled OIDC/SAML provider -> Brisa WSO2 connection, verified claims and optional approved JIT; Brisa-only app roles scoped independently; IdP outage denies new sign-in and business writes fail closed. | **PENDING:** real IdP trace, unique binding proof and Aurora denial. B2B IT. KPI: SSO success, provisioning accuracy, orphaned account count. |
| **Customer privacy preferences** | Marketing communications must reflect explicit current purpose/version consent | On supported clone, WSO2 consent v2 purpose+version, explicit choice and revocation; backend checks current WSO2 state before downstream purpose-specific processing; decisions audited. | **BLOCKED on 7.3.0.12+ and approval.** DPO & Marketing Ops. KPI: time-to-effective-revocation, valid proof coverage, cross-tenant denials. |
| **Lost passkey device** | Passwordless login must not strand legitimate users | WSO2 My Account FIDO2 enrollment; WebAuthn login; credential revoke and approved recovery, no local private key; consistent RP ID/origin. | **PENDING:** device/browser/WSO2 evidence for enroll/login/cancel/revoke/recover. IAM Operations. KPI: passkey adoption, successful recovery rate, revoked-credential denial. |

**Story status rule:** narratives are product designs until they have both real WSO2-backed positive and negative runtime proof. The new `/p2/advanced` page deliberately reflects that distinction.
